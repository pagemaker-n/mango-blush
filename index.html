A few details

Good to know.

Questions about the scent? Start here.

What does Mango Blush smell like?

It opens with fresh mango, moves into jasmine petals, and finishes with warm amber.

How should I apply it?

Spray lightly onto pulse points, such as the wrists or neck. Let it dry naturally without rubbing.

Can I wear it every day?

Its fruity opening and warm floral-amber finish make it an easy choice for daytime or evening wear.
